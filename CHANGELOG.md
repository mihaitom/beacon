# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [0.0.0] - unreleased

Beacon is a from-scratch Vue rebuild of the Feishin Connect desktop client — same casting backend, a leaner, more focused frontend. Nothing has shipped yet; this is the first look at what's here so far.

### Added
- **Sign in with your Navidrome server** and stay signed in across restarts.
- **Browse your library** — Albums, Artists, Tracks, Genres, Playlists, Favorites, and search, all with local filtering across the whole library, not just what's currently loaded.
- **A proper Home** — quick access to what you've been listening to, your most played tracks, recently added albums, and something new to try.
- **Local playback** — queue, shuffle, repeat, seek, volume, and starring tracks, plus a fullscreen Now Playing view.
- **Casting to Sonos, AirPlay, Chromecast, and DLNA devices** — including casting to several at once, taking over a device someone else is using (with a confirmation prompt), and per-device volume control.
- **AirPlay 2 pairing** for devices (HomePods, Apple TVs) that require it.
- **Internet radio stations** — add, play, and manage your own.
- **German and English UI**, detected automatically from your system language and switchable anytime in Settings.
- **A "What's new" dialog** (this one) showing the changelog for the current and past versions, opened automatically after an update or anytime from Settings.
